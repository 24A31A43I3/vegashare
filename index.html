How Client-Side Image & PDF Compression Works

Discover the engineering principles behind VegaShare's lossy & lossless compression engine.

Compressing 5 MB Images to 40 KB Limits

Squeezing large high-resolution photos down to strict application portal limits (such as 20 KB to 50 KB) requires intelligent dimension re-scaling and quantizing algorithms. VegaShare automatically downsamples giant $4000 \times 3000\text{px}$ canvas spaces while converting colors to modern WebP encoding.

By performing binary-search iterative compression directly in HTML5 canvas memory, file sizes drop by over $99\%$ while maintaining text and face clarity.

Client-Side Security & Zero Uploads

Standard cloud converters require uploading your confidential documents or bank statements to remote servers, creating privacy risks. VegaShare processes your files directly within your browser's local memory using JavaScript vector optimization.

Your compressed files never touch external servers during media optimization, keeping your private data 100% confidential.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about VegaShare.

How long are my files stored on VegaShare?

You have full control over file lifespan. Choose storage times ranging from 15 minutes up to 24 hours. You can also configure 'One-Time Access' to instantly delete files after the recipient completes the download.

How do I compress a 5MB image down to 40KB?

Simply select the Compressor tab, set the target size slider to 40 KB, select WebP or JPEG format, and upload your file. Our engine downscales dimensions and tunes quality to fit your target byte size.

Are my compressed images uploaded to external servers?

No! Unlike conventional cloud converters, VegaShare processes PDF and image compression entirely inside your web browser. Your files never touch our servers during compression, ensuring 100% confidentiality.

What is the file size limit for uploads?

VegaShare allows single or batch file uploads up to 200MB per session completely free of charge. For text notes and clipboard transfers, there is virtually no limitation.
