Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Data Collection

VegaShare is designed for anonymity. We do not require accounts or store personal information like names, IP addresses in logs, or emails.

2. Temporary Storage

All uploaded text and files are stored temporarily on our secure servers and are automatically deleted after 30 minutes. Once deleted, data cannot be recovered.

3. Advertisements

We use third-party advertising companies (such as Adsterra) to serve ads. These companies may use non-personal information about your visits to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.
